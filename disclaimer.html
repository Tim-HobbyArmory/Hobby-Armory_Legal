4. Where the data comes from

Set contents, datasheets, points, rules and paint details are compiled from publicly available sources, in particular:

  • manufacturers' public product pages and catalogues,
  • open community projects such as BSData, in line with their licences,
  • publicly available rules references and published rulebooks,
  • corrections and photos submitted by users.
